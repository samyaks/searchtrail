Send Everlaw only what matters. Show exactly how you chose it.

How it works

Four commands from scoping to review

You describe the matter once: custodians, sites and filters. culltool builds the case and the search in Purview, and keeps them in step with your file as you refine it.

01 · DEFINE

Describe the matter

Custodians, extra SharePoint sites, keywords, dates, item types, domains and sensitivity labels, in one matter file. Typos and impossible filters are rejected before anything reaches Microsoft.

culltool kql matter.json
02 · PREVIEW

See what you'd collect

Counts, then an item-level breakdown by custodian, type, sensitivity label and month, plus a spreadsheet of every item. Built from Purview's own report, so it matches the export.

culltool preview matter.json
03 · TEST TERMS

Find the noisy terms

Hits for each search term within all your other filters, so you can see which term is pulling in volume before you agree to it.

culltool terms matter.json
04 · EXPORT

Hand off to Everlaw

One confirmed export from Purview, named for the matter, ready to pick in Everlaw's Purview Cloud Connector. It only runs if the set matches your last preview.

culltool export matter.json --confirm
See the KQL culltool wrote for the example matter 
("project falcon" OR invoic* OR kickback OR "side letter") AND ((kind:email AND ((sent>=2024-01-01 AND sent<=2025-06-30) OR (received>=2024-01-01 AND received<=2025-06-30)) AND NOT participants:newsletters.contoso.com) OR ((kind:microsoftteams OR kind:im) AND ((sent>=2024-01-01 AND sent<=2025-06-30) OR (received>=2024-01-01 AND received<=2025-06-30)) AND NOT participants:newsletters.contoso.com) OR (kind:docs AND (lastmodifiedtime>=2024-01-01 AND lastmodifiedtime<=2025-06-30) AND (fileextension:docx OR fileextension:xlsx OR fileextension:pptx OR fileextension:pdf))) AND NOT InformationProtectionLabelId:11111111-1111-4111-8111-111111111111 AND NOT "weekly digest"

Defensibility

The export can't drift from what you reviewed

Before any export starts, culltool checks it against your last preview. If anything changed, it stops and tells you what, before it touches Purview.

Purview does the collection

culltool never downloads document contents. The export goes from Purview to Everlaw through Everlaw's own connector.

Preview equals export

Previews come from Purview's report on the same search that gets exported, not from a separate search engine.

Filters land where they belong

Domain filters touch emails and chats only; file types touch documents only. Adding a domain never silently drops every document.

Labels include sublabels

Choose "Confidential" and you also get Confidential/Finance and Confidential/Legal, because that's what files actually carry.

A methodology you can share

Every matter produces a plain-English statement of the culling method, ready for a meet-and-confer or a methodology memo.

A log that shows tampering

Previews, term reports and exports are logged with the query, filters, counts and the person who ran them, in a hash chain that verify-log checks.

What's available

Today, and what's next

Available today

  • Command-line tool for Python 3.10 and later
  • Builds Purview eDiscovery cases, custodians, data sources and searches from one matter file
  • Counts and item-level previews, with a spreadsheet of every item
  • Search term hit report
  • Guarded export for Everlaw's Purview Cloud Connector
  • Plain-English methodology statement and tamper-evident culling log
  • Sign in as yourself, or run unattended with an app certificate
  • Offline dry-run mode that shows every call it would make

Coming next

  • Web interface with live counts as you change filters
  • Unique-hits term report and an Excel export for term negotiations
  • Item-level differences between query versions
  • Faster term reports by counting terms in parallel
  • Sensitivity label names inside Everlaw
  • Volume and cost forecasts before export
Where it stands culltool is in early access. It is built and tested end to end against a simulated Purview environment, with 49 automated tests. It has not yet run in a live Microsoft 365 tenant, which is what our first pilots are for.
