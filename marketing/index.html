How it works

From criteria to review in four steps

SearchTrail works with your organization's own Microsoft Purview. It builds the eDiscovery case and search for you, and keeps them in step as you refine your criteria.

01 · SET UP

Choose who and when

Add the people and shared sites in scope and the date range. SearchTrail creates the Purview eDiscovery case.

02 · PREVIEW

See what you'd collect

Counts and an item list by custodian, type, sensitivity label and month, before anything is exported. Document contents stay in Purview.

03 · NARROW

Narrow with numbers

Hits for each search term within all your other settings, for negotiating terms or narrowing a broad request.

04 · SEND

Send it to review

One confirmed Purview export, built only if it matches your last preview. Pick it up through your review tool's Purview connector if it has one, or download the files and load them into Everlaw, Relativity or any other eDiscovery tool.

Search term counts

Bring numbers to the scoping conversation

Record of search

The record behind every search

If your search is questioned by opposing counsel, a regulator, an auditor or a requester, you may need to describe exactly how you searched. SearchTrail writes it down at every step, so you're not reconstructing it months later.

Every search, in plain English

Terms, custodians, locations, dates and counts, written as a statement you can adapt for a declaration or a methodology memo.

Who searched, and when

Every preview, term count and export is logged with the person who ran it. You sign in as yourself, so Purview's own audit log names the same person.

Edits don't go unnoticed

Each log entry carries a fingerprint of the one before it, so a changed or deleted entry is detected.

The export matches the preview

Exports come from the same Purview search you previewed. If the search or the data changed since, SearchTrail stops and tells you what changed.

Nothing quietly skipped

Files Purview couldn't keyword-search, such as encrypted or oversized ones, are counted and kept for review unless you decide otherwise, and that decision is logged.

Your judgment, documented

SearchTrail doesn't decide whether a search is adequate or defensible. It gives you an accurate record to draw on.

Security

Runs inside your organization

SearchTrail runs in your own environment and signs in with your own Microsoft account. There's no new cloud service holding your records.

Document contents never pass through SearchTrail. Exports are built in Purview and go to your review tool or your download folder. SearchTrail reads item details such as names, dates and subjects for previews, and those stay in your environment.

It uses permissions you already manage. Searches run under your Purview role, and an administrator approves one app registration.

What you need

  • Microsoft 365 with Purview eDiscovery (Premium), included in E5 and G5 licenses
  • A review tool that accepts Purview exports, or somewhere to download them
  • Someone with the eDiscovery Manager role in Purview
  • An administrator to approve one app registration

Government clouds (GCC, GCC High and DoD): we're confirming support with our first users.
